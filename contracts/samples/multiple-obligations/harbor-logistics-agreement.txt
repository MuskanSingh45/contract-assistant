MASTER LOGISTICS SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Master Logistics Services Agreement is effective as of January 1, 2026 between Harbor Freight Partners Inc. ("Customer") and Bluewater Logistics Ltd. ("Provider").

1. TERM

1.1 Term. This Agreement commences on the effective date and expires on December 31, 2028. This Agreement will not renew automatically.

1.2 Termination for Convenience. Either party may terminate this Agreement for convenience upon sixty (60) days' written notice to the other party.

2. PROVIDER OBLIGATIONS

2.1 Invoicing. Provider shall deliver an invoice to Customer within five (5) days after the end of each calendar month.

2.2 Business Reviews. Provider shall hold a business review meeting with Customer within fifteen (15) days after the end of each calendar quarter.

2.3 Audit Report. Provider shall deliver the annual compliance audit report to Customer on or before March 1, 2027.

2.4 Insurance. Provider shall maintain cargo insurance of at least USD 5,000,000 throughout the term of this Agreement.

2.5 Security Incidents. Provider shall notify Customer of any security incident within seventy-two (72) hours of discovery.

2.6 Suspension. Provider may suspend services if any invoice remains unpaid for sixty (60) days after its due date.

3. CUSTOMER OBLIGATIONS

3.1 Payment. Customer shall pay each undisputed invoice within thirty (30) days of receipt.

3.2 Forecasts. Customer shall provide Provider with a rolling twelve-month volume forecast at the start of each calendar quarter.

3.3 Inspection. Customer may inspect Provider's warehouse facilities upon ten (10) days' prior notice.
