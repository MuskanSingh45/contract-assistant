CONSULTING AGREEMENT

This Consulting Agreement is effective as of June 1, 2026 between Meridian Health Partners ("Client") and Brightline Advisory Group ("Consultant").

1. Services

1.1 Consultant shall provide the advisory services described in the statement of work agreed by the parties from time to time.

2. Duration

2.1 This Agreement continues until the Services are completed to the reasonable satisfaction of the Client.

2.2 Either party may end this Agreement upon reasonable notice to the other party.

3. Reporting

3.1 Consultant shall deliver a project status report to Client every two weeks while Services are being performed.

4. Cooperation

4.1 Client shall provide timely access to relevant personnel and information.

5. Fees

5.1 Fees are payable within the period agreed with the relevant department.
