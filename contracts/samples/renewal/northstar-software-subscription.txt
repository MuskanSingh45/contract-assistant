SOFTWARE SUBSCRIPTION AGREEMENT

This Software Subscription Agreement is effective as of March 15, 2026 (the "Effective Date") between Northstar Inc. ("Subscriber") and Cloud Platform Ltd. ("Vendor").

1. SUBSCRIPTION

1.1 Access. Vendor shall make the Northstar analytics platform available to Subscriber with 99.9% monthly availability.

2. TERM AND RENEWAL

2.1 Initial Term. The initial term of this Agreement is two (2) years from the Effective Date.

2.2 Renewal. Upon expiry of the initial term, this Agreement shall automatically renew for successive one (1) year terms unless either party gives written notice of non-renewal at least thirty (30) business days before the end of the then-current term.

3. FEES

3.1 Subscription Fee. Subscriber shall pay the annual subscription fee on each anniversary of the Effective Date.

4. REPORTING

4.1 Uptime Report. Vendor shall deliver an uptime service report to Subscriber within ten (10) days after the end of each calendar month.
