HOSTING AGREEMENT

1. Parties

This Hosting Agreement is made on January 1, 2025 between Globex Inc. ("Client") and Initech Hosting LLC ("Provider").

2. Services

2.1 Provider shall host and maintain the Client's web applications with 99.5% monthly availability.

3. Term

3.2 Renewal. The Agreement expires on December 31, 2026 and renews automatically for one (1) year unless terminated by written notice given at least ninety (90) days prior to expiration.

12. Notices

12.1 Notices. Any notice of non-renewal must be delivered no later than sixty (60) days before the expiration date.
